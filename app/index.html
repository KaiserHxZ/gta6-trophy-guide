Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 for difficulty and about 200 hours. One playthrough is enough, there are no difficulty settings, and cheats disable trophies, so keep the run cheat-free.

  1. 1 Play the story 60–80 hours

    Finish the 5 chapters and enjoy the game. Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through the binoculars as you meet them — progress counts toward the 100% checklist even before you can see it. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter; there is no mission replay.

  2. 2 The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: only 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, and 70 of 80 challenges are needed. The only things that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests.

  3. 3 Cleanup about 10 hours

    Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands and pick up the miscellaneous trophies left over. Every trophy quest giver returns after the story, so nothing is locked out.

Watch out: one possibly missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. It raises the counter but is not marked as a spot, and PowerPyx notes it is still unconfirmed whether it is required. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe.
